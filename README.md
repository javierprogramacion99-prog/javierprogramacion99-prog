## 🚀 Featured project — 4Free

**ForFree** is a Flutter + Firebase marketplace app for Chile (iOS / Android / Web) that connects people to give and receive things for free. **SELKNAM (SELK)** is its Ethereum ERC-20 token — backed by the identity of the Selk'nam people — powering the ForFree ecosystem.

> 🇪🇸 **ForFree** es una app marketplace en Flutter + Firebase para Chile que conecta a personas para dar y recibir cosas gratis. **SELKNAM (SELK)** es su token ERC-20 en Ethereum — inspirado en la identidad del pueblo selk'nam — que impulsa el ecosistema ForFree.
